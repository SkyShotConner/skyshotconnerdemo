ABOUT SKYSHOTCONNER

Where aviation meets the lens.

SkyShotConner is an aviation photography project based in South Africa, built around a simple idea: every aircraft has a story worth capturing.

From airliners arriving over Johannesburg to helicopters, historic aircraft and golden-hour departures, the goal is to document aviation with a balance of technical detail and visual storytelling.
