BEHIND THE LENS ✦

ABOUT SKYSHOTCONNER

Where aviation meets the lens.

SkyShotConner is the aviation photography project of Conner, a South African aviation enthusiast and photographer with a passion for aircraft, airports and the people who keep aviation moving.

What started with a fascination for aviation has grown into a journey of photographing the aircraft and aviation communities that make South Africa's skies so unique. From commercial airliners and general aviation to helicopters, historic aircraft and military aviation, every subject has a story worth preserving.

The goal is simple: capture the atmosphere, precision and energy of aviation and build a visual record that gets better with every frame.
