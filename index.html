ABOUT SKYSHOTCONNER

Where aviation meets the lens.

SkyShotConner is the aviation photography project of Conner, a South African aviation enthusiast and photographer with a passion for aircraft, airports and the people who keep aviation moving.

What started with a fascination for aviation has grown into a journey of photographing the aircraft and aviation communities that make South Africa's skies so unique. From commercial airliners and general aviation to helicopters, historic aircraft and military aviation, every subject has a story worth preserving.

Conner's photography is about more than simply recording an aircraft. It is about capturing the atmosphere of a flight, the precision of aviation, the energy of an airshow and the moments that make people stop and look up. SkyShotConner also celebrates the wider aviation world — from pilots and air traffic control to engineers, aircrews, airport teams and the aircraft themselves.

Through the SkyShotConner gallery and Aircraft Archive, the goal is to build a visual record of aviation while continuing to learn, explore new airports and improve with every frame.
